One lighting scene for your PC and room.

Carry an effect from your keyboard to the lights behind your monitor and across the room. Keep media and temperatures on a compatible LCD.

Get Windows Setup Check my devices

The download page lists the files for the release. Choose Prismestra-Setup-…-x64.exe for Windows 10 or 11.

Setup includes OpenRGB, a background component that controls compatible PC lights.

Your first scene

  1. Install Prismestra and open Devices to see what it found.
  2. Pick a scene. Move devices on the canvas if you want the effect to follow their positions.
  3. Close the editor when you are done. The scene keeps running from the system tray.
