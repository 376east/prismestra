One lighting scene for your PC and room.

Carry an effect from your keyboard to the lights behind your monitor and across the room. Keep media and temperatures on a compatible LCD.

Get Windows Setup Check my devices

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). All release files

Setup includes OpenRGB, a background component that controls compatible PC lights.

Your first scene

  1. Run the installer and open Prismestra. It searches for devices and starts a scene on compatible lights it can set up automatically.
  2. Keyboard lit but your strip is still dark? Open Devices to see what is connected, still being detected, or needs setup. Once connected, use Find devices when search is available.
  3. To change the effect, open Scenes, pick one and click Apply. You can close the editor and leave the lights running.
