One lighting scene for your PC and room.

Run the same effect on your keyboard, LED strips and the lights behind your monitor. Show an image, animation or temperatures on a compatible LCD.

Get Windows Setup Check my devices

Windows 10 version 1903 or later, or Windows 11 (x64). All release files

OpenRGB comes with Prismestra. One installer is all you need.

Your first scene

  1. Run the installer and open Prismestra. It starts a scene on the lights it sets up automatically.
  2. If a light stays dark, open Devices to see what connected and what needs setup.
  3. To change the effect, open Scenes, pick one and click Apply. You can close the editor and leave the lights running.
